How Review Commissions & Referral Splits Work

Interactive preview demonstrating 1:1 commission matching and network split rules.

What You're Paying For

Every approved review generates two amounts: a reward to the reviewer (the amount you set below) and a matching platform fee to Echo Hub (the same amount again) which funds the workspace, tools, and infrastructure you use to run your campaigns.

About inviter splits: If you were invited to Echo Hub by someone, or the reviewer was, part of the platform fee is shared with that inviter as an affiliate bonus. The reviewer's reward is never reduced by this — they get 100% of what you set.

How to test your setup: Use the slider below to select the commission you plan to payout per review, and pick a referral scenario you might face. The tool will calculate the breakdown of the review commission and the commission paid to the platform (which is split if needed). Moving the slider also automatically sets your official commission input on the form below!

You pay zero listing fees or monthly subscriptions—you only pay when a reviewer submits verified feedback. Your configured seller's commission payout creates a 1:1 match disbursed across the reviewer payout and the referral network pool.

Understanding Campaign Types

Paid vs Product-Only — which one is right for you?

Every campaign has a Campaign Type — the rules for what the shopper receives when their review is approved. Pick a default now, override per product later.

Paid Campaign

  • Shopper gets a cash reward per approved review
  • Shopper buys the product at your retail price
  • More shoppers claim paid campaigns

Best for: competitive niches and high-value items.

Product-Only Campaign

  • Shopper gets the product for free and keeps it
  • You cover shipping the product to the shopper
  • No cash reward — cheaper per review

Best for: low-cost items, samples, apparel.

What's the same in both

  • You always pay the Agent / Platform Fee per approved review — even on product-only.
  • The fee is split between the Agent who runs Echo Hub and any friends who invited you or your shopper.
  • Referral bonuses are paid the same way on both campaign types.
  • You're only charged when a review is approved. Not on inquiry, not on claim.

You can mix both types on one account

Your choice here becomes your default. New products will use it — but you can override the type per product any time.
